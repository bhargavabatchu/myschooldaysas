name: Campus Live fee collection reporting
description: Business definitions for daily payment collection totals and channel breakdowns.
---

Daily collections are grouped by the date the school received the payment, not the invoice issue date. Manual cash is cash; manual UPI and bank transfer plus Razorpay are digital/online; cheque remains separate; mock gateway payments are test payments and must never be presented as real online receipts.

**Why:** the user wants daily cash-versus-online visibility, and demo/mock payments must not be mistaken for actual collections.

**How to apply:** show a dated transaction ledger with receipt and collector details, keep test payments identifiable, and only expose school-wide collection reporting to school administrators.
