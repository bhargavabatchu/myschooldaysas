---
name: Campus Live fee concessions
description: Product rules for manually granted, student-specific fee concessions.
---

For the MVP, issue the standard fee first, then let an administrator set a fixed concession on one student's invoice. Keep the original fee amount and recorded payments unchanged; the concession reduces only the unpaid balance. Do not automatically apply it to other students or future invoices. Keep the reason administrator-only and record who changed it.

**Why:** the user specified a post-issuance, per-student adjustment and chose not to change recorded payment history or create an account credit.

**How to apply:** keep concessions invoice-scoped and administrator-controlled. Show the family the concession and current balance, never the internal reason. Reject any amount that would reduce the balance below recorded payments.
