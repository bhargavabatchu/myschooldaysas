---
name: Campus Live student withdrawal
description: Preserve student records and account access when an enrollment is withdrawn.
---

Withdrawing a student ends active enrollment records with a withdrawal reason; it does not delete the student account, profile, fees, attendance, results, or sign-in access. Administrators may restore the most recent withdrawn enrollment only when its class and section remain active and have capacity.

**Why:** The user chose archive/withdraw rather than deletion and expects school history to remain available. Disabling access is a separate decision and must not happen implicitly.

**How to apply:** Keep withdrawal distinct from hard deletion and account deactivation. Make inactive students visible only to administrators, and require confirmation before withdrawal or restoration.

Administrators can edit a student's name, sign-in email, current admission number, demographic details, and guardian contact details. Class and section changes use enrollment transfer; changing the current admission number must not rewrite historical enrollments.

**Why:** The user requested admin access to correct all student details while preserving academic history.

**How to apply:** Enforce school-level email and active academic-year admission-number uniqueness. Treat guardian contact fields as contact information, not as a guardian account or access link.
