---
name: Campus Live academic years
description: Central school-year setup, statuses, and selection across admin workflows.
---

Campus Live uses a centrally registered list of academic years per school. Keep one year marked current, future years planned, and prior years completed. Admin workflows should select from this list rather than accept unrelated free-text year labels. Changing the current year updates defaults and the annual dashboard summary; it must not silently move students or modify existing invoices.

**Why:** the user chose central year setup and selectors so administrators can manage the school calendar once and use consistent year values throughout the app.

**How to apply:** add and change years from the Academic calendar page, then use the shared list in admissions, enrollment, promotion, and fee issuance. Preserve historical enrollment and payment records when a year changes.
